## Description (paste into the page)

**Terra Bellum is a turn-based grand strategy game on a living globe.** Pick an era, pick a nation, and write its chronicle: raise armies, sign pacts and break them, marry into dynasties, trade, scheme, and outlast every other court in the world.

**Thirteen eras, one world.** Begin in Ancient Rome, the Mongol steppe, the Age of Discovery, Napoleon's Europe, the trenches of 1914, the Second World War, the Cold War, or today. Every era has its own map, its own rulers, its own events.

**Every nation, every province.** About 250 nations and nearly 1,800 provinces on a globe you can spin or flatten. Develop districts, build, tax, recruit, march armies across land and sea, and watch supply and rebellion bite.

**A court that thinks.** The AI rulers have temperaments (conquerors, merchants, diplomats, guardians, opportunists), long memories and grudges. They answer requests with their own interests: alliances, non-aggression pacts, royal marriages, trade deals, ultimatums, coalitions against the aggressor, spies at the gate.

**Decide with your eyes open.** An Advisor flags crises and opportunities, every figure explains itself, and the interface scales for readability: text sizes, colour-blind modes, high contrast, reduced motion, screen-reader announcements, touch targets.

**Plays offline.** No account, no internet, no ads, no purchases inside the game. Autosaves every turn; five save slots.

**Multiplayer is coming soon.** Online games for several players are being prepared and need a server that is not open yet. Single player is complete.

### Controls
* **Mouse:** click a province to inspect it; drag to turn the globe; wheel to zoom. **Touch:** tap, drag and pinch.
* **Keyboard:** `Enter` presses the seal (end turn; `Ctrl+Enter` ends it without the warning), `N` or `F1` nations, `B` or `F2` budget, `D` or `F3` decrees, `F4` advisor, `Y` or `F5` annals, `F6` goals, `L` map lenses, `A` jump between alerts, `+` / `-` zoom, arrow keys move the map cursor, `Home` returns to your capital, `V` province list, `Esc` back or menu.
* The seal at the bottom right ends your turn. Each turn is six months.

### System requirements
* **Windows:** Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), OpenGL 3.3 graphics, 2 GB RAM, 130 MB disk. The game is not code-signed: if Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose *More info → Run anyway*.
* **Android:** Android 5.0 or newer (phones and tablets, landscape), about 80 MB. The APK is installed outside Google Play: allow *Install unknown apps* for your browser or the itch app once.

### Saves
Your campaign is saved automatically at the end of every turn; **Continue** on the title resumes it. Saves live on your device only.
