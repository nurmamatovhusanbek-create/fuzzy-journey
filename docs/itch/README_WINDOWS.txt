TERRA BELLUM 1.0.0 - Windows
=============================
Unzip anywhere and run TerraBellum.exe. No installer, no internet needed.

Needs: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), a graphics card or driver with OpenGL 3.3 (any PC from the last 10 years),
2 GB of RAM. Saves and settings are kept in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Terra Bellum.

Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" because the game is not code-signed:
choose "More info" and then "Run anyway".

Multiplayer is coming soon. Single player is complete: 13 eras, every nation, AI courts.
