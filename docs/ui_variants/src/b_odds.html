Likelihood — variations

Five ways to roll, and two odds-only bars (6 and 7) for the next-turn answers. Together: seven ways to show "how likely is this?" in the Bezel language: thin brass instruments, hatched refusal zones, engraved diamonds. Cards 1 to 5 play the same roll when you press Roll (the dice are cast once for all); cards 6 and 7 only show the chance. Move the odds slider to see low and high chances.
