Your four navies

Your four navies, each as the "navy" of the palette: the plates, the decline end of the bar, the You tint on the map. Everything else is unchanged (brass gold, #B31B1B red). Each navy is dark enough that on its own it barely separates from the near-black panel, so every card derives a brighter info blue for icons and chips and a still brighter tint for small blue text; the numbers on each card give the contrast of all three.
