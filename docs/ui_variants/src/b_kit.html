Bezel — shape & component kit

One object world: brass bezels, graduated scales, notched plates. Round things are instruments (dials, gauges, buttons that are pressed or turned). Rectangles are documents or plates and have their corners cut, never rounded. Every header ends in a graduated rule. The accent is brass; rust is danger/attention; verdigris is good; sky blue is information.

1 · Anatomy

Bezel (bezelSvg): outer brass ring with a diagonal gradient, a 1-px bright inner edge, a lit arc at the upper left and a shaded arc at the lower right (reads as a turned metal ring), and a graduated scale of ticks on the face: every fifth tick is long. The face is a near-black umber.

Sizes: 24 px ticks only (rail), 31 px (rail button), 52 px (gauge), 66 px (medallion), 108 px (chronometer). The ring is always ≈ 9 % of the radius.

Used for: nation medallion, resource gauges, date dial, menu button, rail buttons, minimap, End Turn chronometer, radial command ring, knobs, goals, advisors.

4 · Plates, headers, rule

Plate: a rectangle with 10-px chamfered corners (8 for tags, 12 for large panels) and a 1-px brass-dim hairline made with two nested clip-paths, an inner 3-px dark inset and a faint brass line inside it. Drop shadow comes from a wrapper because clip-path would cut it.

Header: 46-px bezel icon, Cinzel 22 px title, italic subtitle, 36-px round close button, then the graduated rule (ticks every 8 px, long tick every 40 px). It is the one repeating ornament.

Tuner tabs: tab labels sit above a tick strip; the active tab has a red pointer, like a radio dial.
