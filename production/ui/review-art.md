The harness should hide the HUD for pick scenes.

## Verdict rationale

The material language is right and distinctive: chamfered dark furniture, pale flat documents, brass only as accent, one dressed hero sheet. Near-zoom gonfalons, the hover tooltip, alert chips, resource chips and the hero event and game-over sheets are close to the bible and are the best work in the build. It does not read as generic. The top bar, dock, chips and alert stack read as a HoI4 strip, and the near-tier banners read as Total War.

It is not ready, for these reasons:

1. **Pillar 2 is not visible on the map.** The orders arrow that should carry the "orders live on the map" idea is effectively absent in every armed, preview and committed shot (B-1).
2. **The squint and greyscale test (principle 3) fails on every paper screen.** The brass primary is indistinguishable from the controls around it, while the black selected segments and chips are the heaviest objects on the page (A-1, A-2).
3. **Several things the bible removes or fixes are still present or inconsistent.** These are the selection pulse and yellow wash, the portrait medallion, the non-circular End Turn "seal", the legacy raw colours, colour emoji and contradictory ▲/▼ glyphs (A-3 to A-12).
4. **The title screen, the part the user loves, has regressed.** The globe disc was 0.50 alpha and is now 0.78. A 594 px (1280 wide) to 750 px (1920 wide) slab of plates sits over the globe, and the bible's text rows became plates (A-8).

## Gestalt

- **Command table, yes. Paper-ink-brass, yes.**
  - On the map layer it reads as a staff table at night: dark chips and rail, cream and brass text, flat chamfers.
  - On the document layer (Nations, Settings, Saves) it reads as a tablet settings app. The cause is full-width segmented bars with black fills, 56 px stepper squares, bordered chips inside bordered cards, and a 14 px brown scrollbar. This is not "Total War / HoI4 command table".
- **Too bold** (the player's earlier complaint is only half fixed):
  - Black selected segments and chips (A-2).
  - 2 px brass-ink borders on earned Honours cards.
  - The 14 px full-height scrollbar.
  - Box-in-box stacking: card border, then cost chip border, then button border (Decisions, Saves).
- **Too decorative:**
  - The ruler portrait medallion with its saturated crimson and navy wash. It appears in 5 screens, which is more than any other element (A-9).
  - Diamond overuse (I-9).
  - The title bezel plus 78 % disc plus four bordered plates (A-8).
  - The kit specimen seal, a gradient wax disc with a rim, which still ships as dead code (A-4).
- **Greyscale** (3 px blur, see `/tmp/claude-0/ad/grey.png`):
  - Wax-red End Turn collapses to a dark slab and reads only through its brass ring.
  - Brass fill (L* about 71) against paper-1 (L* about 84) is about 1.3:1 in luminance. "Show on map", "Search…" and the tab band read as one tone.
  - Black segments dominate Rankings, where there is no primary at all.
  - On the map, own gonfalons hold up, but under the Economic lens (gold ramp) the brass "own" outline loses separation.

## What passes (do not regress)

- Token source: `ui_tokens.gd` matches 4.2-4.5 hex-for-hex, with the HC table and a registered contrast pair list.
- Flat PLATE, no ornament rules on panels (D3). Hero-only grain, ragged edge, guards and ornament (D1, D2, D4): the plain panels in Nations, Council and Settings carry none.
- Top bar, resource chips, alert stack and tooltip follow 7.5. Top-bar chips use semantic glyph plus sign plus colour (▲ +798, ⚠ capacity).
- Near-tier gonfalon: number on paper, band, swallowtail, brass own outline, "+n" tab, spent greyed.
- Orders-arrow code structure (casing, 4 px core, double chevron, dashed preview). The geometry is right; B-1 is trim and ordering only.
- Title wordmark (Cinzel 900) and bezel ring present and turning.
- Greyscale-safe tooltip and alert-chip shape code (▲ crisis, ▲ outline warning, ◆ offer, ● info).

2. Primary versus secondary tone (4.5 and 7.5): add a non-hue carrier (A-1).
3. Slider thumb brass (7.5) against D8 "one brass fill per container": the thumb is a control, not a primary.
4. Seal shape (A-4) and order-card placement (A-10): either the build or the bible changes.
5. Selection and hover colours (6.2) against `globe.gdshader`: the bible rule stands (A-5).
6. 11 px "+n" tab and hotkey cap (I-3): no spec exists; the 12 px floor applies.
