## Verdict reasoning

The desktop (1280x720 / 1920x1080) experience closely implements the specs and is a clear improvement on the old UI. NEEDS REVISION (not MAJOR REVISION) because the failures are layout-budget and profile problems in specific components (S/L and portrait), each with a bounded fix, rather than a flawed design. Four BLOCKERs must be fixed before sign-off; all MAJORs should be fixed or consciously waived by the creative director with the spec amended. Recommended regression gate: extend `tests/ui_modals.gd` and `tests/ui_cmdcard.gd` with rect-intersection assertions (card vs seal, plates vs tools, primary button inside viewport, choices inside viewport) at 800x360, 900x415, 540x960 in EN and RU.
