# Map and orders fixes (branch fix-map)

Stream: ui-programmer + technical-artist. Scope: `godot/src/render/*`, `order_flow.gd`, `map_tip.gd`, new `map_cursor.gd`, `nav_pad.gd`, `map_list.gd`, small hooks in `main.gd`.

## What changed

- **Orders arrow (art B-1).** `map_labels.gd` redraws the arrow per art bible 5.2: 4 px core, 2 px casing each side, head fixed in px (12 px triangle for moves, 14 px double chevron for attacks), dashed preview. The shaft is clipped by arc length between the two marker rims. When the markers leave less than 30 px between them, the target marker (and, for previews, the source marker too) slides outward along the axis, so a visible shaft always remains (asserted >= 20 px by `tests/map_arrows.gd` at four zoom tiers, for the closest, a typical and the widest neighbouring pair, for preview, armed-hover and move arrows). The arrow is drawn above the markers; the strength / turn chip sits beside the shaft on the side with fewer markers and off the HUD keep-outs.
- **One confirm surface (art A-10 / ux m-04).** The on-map preview chip owns Cancel / Attack. The preview chip now chooses its place among eight candidates, scoring overlap with the HUD keep-outs (End Turn, dock, ribbon, card), the arrow with its label, the source marker and the target itself. `TBOrderFlow.summary()` returns the order as data so the command card can show it read-only; `confirm()` / `cancel()` / `is_previewing()` are the only mutators.
- **Selection / hover (art A-5).** `globe.gdshader`: selection is a static +8 % lift with a 3 px cream ring inside and 1 px ink outside (3 + 2 px in high contrast); hover is a 2 px `brass_lt` ring; no pulse, no wash, no `time` uniform at all.
- **Labels (art A-6, CON-006).** Nation names are cream always, never faded, own realm in the heavier Cinzel 900 face, 3 px halo (5 in HC) whose alpha follows the luminance of the land under the label. Collision: markers, HUD rects, the order arrow and higher-priority names; candidates slide along the nation's principal axis (capped to about 12 degrees), shift sideways, wrap to two lines, drop to the first word, else the name is dropped. Your own realm is placed first. Province names keep out of nation names and markers. Fonts rebuilt when `readable` toggles; sizes follow `K.text_scale`; nothing below 12 px.
- **Marker tiers (art A-7, I-2).** Far = 8 px dots (own, at war with you, selected only; war = dot with a red outline, no diamonds). Mid pennants claim rectangles that cover outline + pip, so they cannot overlap. "+n" tabs are drawn last, above every marker, at 12 px. Out-cubic appear, no overshoot, no hover scale.
- **Nation pick (ux M-05).** `map.highlight_nation(n)` lights the whole nation (brightened fill + brass outline) and dims the rest to about 30 %; `map.clear_highlight()` restores. Tap magnet: on the pick screen a tap within 20 dp of a much smaller nation (or of land when the tap hit the sea) snaps to it (`TBMapView._pick_tap`, used automatically while `g.human_id == 0`).
- **Colour-vision modes (CVD-001/002/004/005, D8).** `cfg["cvd"]` in off / deuter / protan / tritan. `TBLenses` keeps the ACTIVE palettes in static vars (`STAB_RAMP`, `REL_COL`, ... so the HUD legend follows unchanged): Okabe-Ito categorical sets, viridis / magma ramps, the art bible 4.6 stability ramp in every mode. Political lens: adjacency-aware greedy colouring over the nation graph (12 hue steps + 2 greys, CIE76 distance under the Machado simulation, one reserved colour for you), stable between turns and re-solved only where owners now clash (`TBMapView.repaint` recomputes on owner and relation change). Relation patterns in screen space (shader, class code 0..5 in the palette flag channel): war = 45 degree hatch on the shared front, ally = double line, own = heavy brass line, truce / NAP = dotted, rebels = cross-hatch; subtle in the standard look, full strength in CVD or HC. Nation names carry a relation glyph (swords, linked rings, hourglass, own ring) in strong mode and a bloc letter in the wars lens. Valid targets get a dotted fill in strong mode. Hover card carries the active lens value in words (`TBLenses.describe`).
- **High-contrast map (CON-005/006/007).** `TBTokens.mode == 1`: 2x borders, black sea, land lifted to a 3:1 floor, milder globe shading, no rim light, 3 px halos with opaque alpha, 3 + 2 px selection.
- **Keyboard and single-pointer routes (KBD-004, GES-001/002).** `TBMapCursor` (installed by one line in `main.gd`): arrow keys move a province cursor to the neighbour nearest in that direction, Enter selects (or orders when the cursor is a lit target), Shift+Enter orders explicitly, Esc drops the cursor, `,` `.` previous / next alert, Shift+arrows pan, `+` `-` zoom, Home recentre, `V` opens the list. The cursor is a static double ring with corner brackets plus the hover card text. `TBNavPad`: pan arrows, zoom + / -, recentre, list; flat 48 px plates, bottom-left, slides clear of the HUD / card, hold-to-repeat, `cfg["navpad"]` auto / on / off (auto = touch screen, text >= 150 % or Large targets). `TBMapList`: searchable Provinces / Armies popover built on `K.modal`; picking a row flies to the province and selects it. `TBMapView.focus_on(p)` and `TBMapList.focus_province(map, p)` expose the "focus a province" call.
- **Reduce motion (MOT).** Every map motion reads `TBKit.motion_ok()`: fly-to and zoom are cuts, no flick inertia, no marching dashes, no selection or hover fades, no marker pop, no title / menu globe auto-spin (`main.gd`), the shader has no animated term.
- **Tokens (art A-11, map part).** No raw `Color(...)` left in `render/`, `order_flow.gd`, `map_tip.gd` or the new files; the battle floaters in `main.gd` use `pos_bar` / `neg_bar`; unit floaters carry a sign glyph.
- **Strings.** `ml_*`, `nav_*` in en / ru / uz.
